June 21, 1905: Birthday of writer and philosopher Jean-Paul Sartre. Jean-Paul Sartre (1905-1980) is best known for his version of existentialism, which he promoted with both scholarly and literary works. His best-known book is Being and Nothingness.
June 22, 1767: Birthday of philosopher and linguist Wilhelm von Humboldt. Wilhelm von Humboldt (1767-1835) founded the Humboldt University in Berlin and contributed to the philosophy of language. He is best known for his generalist ideal of education.
Quote of the day: “There are undeniably certain kinds of knowledge that must be of a general nature and, more importantly, a certain cultivation of the mind and character that nobody can afford to be without.” — Wilhelm von Humboldt
